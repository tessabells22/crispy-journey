    How to use

    AXOs come in from #axo-assignments on their own. You only add your updates.

    1. Pick your name under You at the top. It is remembered on this browser.
    2. Find the AXO you worked on (search by number or title) and click Update, or use Add update at the top.
    3. Pick a status, the shift date and shift, write your notes, and add any test file links. Every update is kept, so the next shift sees the full history.
    4. An update of Passed - Develop/Deployment closes the AXO. Use Reopen in the AXO panel if it needs more work.
    5. If an AXO is missing, copy its Slack post and use Paste AXO.

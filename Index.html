How to use

AXOs come in from #axo-assignments on their own. You only add your updates.

  1. Pick your name at the top right. It is remembered on this browser.
  2. Open the AXO you worked on. Needs a QA lists AXOs nobody has touched yet, and My AXOs lists the ones you have updated.
  3. In the panel, pick a status, check the shift date and shift, write your notes, add any test file links, and save. Every update is kept as history.
  4. Passed - Develop/Deployment closes the AXO. Use Reopen in the panel if it needs more work.
  5. If an AXO is missing, copy its Slack post and use Paste AXO.
